## Introduction
One of the most overlooked yet fundamental aspects of accurate translation lies in ensuring glossary consistency. Whether you're translating for a multinational audience or localizing a niche product, consistent terminology forms the bedrock of effective communication.

## Pain Points
Inconsistent glossary application can lead to:

- Confusion among readers due to varying terminology.
- Mistranslation of critical terms.
- Brand misrepresentation in different languages.

These issues can damage credibility and impact the user experience significantly.
