## Introduction

When translating documents, maintaining the original layout and formatting is as critical as the fidelity of the translation itself. Whether it’s a research paper, a business proposal, or an instruction manual, professional aesthetics play a pivotal role in effective communication.

## Pain Points

- **Formatting Disruption:** Translations often harm the original visual structure, causing disordered content.
- **Glossaries and Tables:** Critical terms and tabular data get mistranslated or misaligned.
- **Time Consumption:** Reformatting documents post-translation is labor-intensive.
