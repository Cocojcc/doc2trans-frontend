In legal, technical, and medical documents, **consistency** often matters more than style. A glossary is the foundation.

## Why glossaries are critical

- Prevent multiple translations of the same term
- Reduce compliance risk and review cost
- Improve reader trust and clarity

## What a good glossary includes

- Source term and approved translation
- Context notes and prohibited variants
- Industry standards or regulatory references

## How to implement

1. Extract terms from legacy documents and client standards
2. Confirm preferred translations with stakeholders
3. Enforce glossary usage during translation
4. Run consistency checks after translation

## FAQ

**Q: Will glossaries hurt fluency?**  
A: No. Properly managed glossaries improve accuracy and consistency.

**Q: What if we don’t have a glossary yet?**  
A: Start with high‑frequency and regulated terms.

**Q: Do glossaries work across languages?**  
A: Yes, they are essential for multilingual alignment.
