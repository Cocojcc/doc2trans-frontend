## Introduction

When translating documents, preserving the original formatting and structure is often as critical as the translation's accuracy. Disruptions in layouts, glossary inconsistencies, or broken tables can lead to confusion, miscommunication, and extra work. 

### The Challenges

- **Formatting Issues**: Table misalignments, image disruptions, and font mismatches.
- **Glossary Inconsistencies**: Translating industry-specific terms with different meanings.
- **Time Consuming**: Manual reformatting post-translation slows down workflows.
