## Introduction
When translating documents, a common challenge faced by individuals and businesses is the loss of the original layout and structure. For professionals dealing with design-heavy content or big data tables, redesigning every translated document is inefficient.

## Pain Points in Manual Layout Preservation
- Recreating layouts increases workload.
- Errors often occur during copy-pasting.
- Special formats like tables or charts lose integrity during translation.
