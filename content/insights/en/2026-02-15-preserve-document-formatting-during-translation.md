## Introduction

Preserving the formatting of a document during translation is crucial to ensure clarity and professional presentation. Many tools alter layouts, resulting in disrupted structures and inefficiencies for businesses. 

## Challenges in Preserving Formatting
- **Complex Structures**: Tables, charts, and multi-column layouts often lose alignment.
- **Font Issues**: Text size and color mismatches can impact readability.
- **Glossary Management**: Inconsistent terminology especially with technical content.
